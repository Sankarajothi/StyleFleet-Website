Quick FAQs

How to add custom categories? ▼

Go to the Price List page and click "+ Add Category". Enter your custom category name (e.g., Bridal, Nails, Spa). It will automatically show up across Price List and the "Import from Menu" billing modal.

How do partial payments work? ▼

When creating a bill, select the "Partial Pay" payment mode. Enter the amount paid now. The remaining balance will automatically be logged under Pending Dues in the Dashboard and CRM.

Can I print bills on thermal printers? ▼

Yes! In the bill modal or receipt view, tap "Print Bill" or use Ctrl+P to trigger ESC/POS or standard thermal 58mm/80mm printing.
